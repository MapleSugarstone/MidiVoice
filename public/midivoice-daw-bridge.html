1 · MIDI output

Looking for MIDI ports…

No virtual MIDI port found. Create one, then Rescan.

  • Windows: install loopMIDI (free), press its + button to create a port.
  • macOS: Audio MIDI Setup > Window > Show MIDI Studio > double-click IAC Driver > tick Device is online.
  • Linux: the built-in Midi Through port works as is.

2 · Reaper

  1. Options > Preferences > Audio > MIDI Inputs. Enable input on your virtual port. Older Reapers list it under MIDI Devices.
  2. Insert > Virtual instrument on new track. Pick a synth.
  3. Right-click the track's record arm button. Input: MIDI > your port > All channels.
  4. Press Send test note above. The synth should sound.
  5. Record in Reaper. Sing here.

Other DAWs work the same. Enable the port as a MIDI input and arm a track.

Notes land about a tenth of a second after you sing them. Nudge the recorded items back in Reaper, or record in the full app for corrected timing.

Keep this tab open. It keeps sending while Reaper has focus.
